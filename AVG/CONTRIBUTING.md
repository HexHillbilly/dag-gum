# Contributing to AVG

Contributions to the Active Variety Governor (AVG) are accepted under the
GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPLv3); see [LICENSE](LICENSE).

By submitting a contribution, you agree that your contribution may also be
distributed under the project's commercial license, which preserves the
owner's ability to dual-license AVG.

For large changes, open an issue first to discuss the design and roadmap fit
before submitting a pull request.
